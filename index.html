Emergency Overview

On April 27, 2025, at 21:45 local time, a 6.5 magnitude earthquake struck Paradoxia, with the epicenter located 20km offshore from the southwestern coast. The earthquake, combined with tsunami waves, has caused widespread devastation across southwestern coastal regions and mountainous areas.

Current estimates indicate 11,000 deaths, 20,000+ injuries, and 600,000 people left homeless. Critical infrastructure including health facilities, schools, roads, and water systems have been severely damaged. Approximately 2.1 million people require urgent humanitarian assistance.

Priority Areas:

  • Southwestern coastal regions (epicenter area)
  • Southern mountain areas with landslides
  • Four refugee camps (A1-A4) in the southeast
  • Areas with damaged petrol storage facilities
